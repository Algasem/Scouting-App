# FRC Team 7476 Scouting App 

The 7476 Scouting App enables efficient collection and management of scouting data for FIRST Robotics Competition events. 
It supports both match scouting and pit scouting, with offline data collection and syncing to Google Sheets.

## Installation

### Android 
1. Open https://earl-of-march-frc.github.io/2026-Scouting-App/ in Safari
2. Tap the 3 dots, scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen", then tap Add
3. The app will appear as an icon on your home screen

### iOS 
1. Open https://earl-of-march-frc.github.io/2026-Scouting-App/ in Safari
2. Tap the Share button, scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen", then tap Add
3. The app will appear as an icon on your home screen

### PC
- Open https://earl-of-march-frc.github.io/2026-Scouting-App/ in any browser
- Note: An internet connection is currently required. Offline PC usage is in development.

## First Time Setup
1. Open the app 
2. Select your name from the "Scout" dropdown at the top
3. Your name will be saved automatically for future sessions

## Usage

### Match Scouting
Fill out the following sections during each match:
- Before Match: Event, Match Stage, Driver Station, Match #, Team #, Scout Name 
- Auto: Climb Level, Alliance Auto Points, Robot's Contribution, Fuel Missed, Balls Collected
- Teleop: Alliance Teleop Points, Robot's Contribution, Fuel Missed, Traversal, Phase Roles, Fouls, Collaboration, Driving Skill
- Endgame: Climb Level, Climb Speed, Disconnect 
- Notes: Important observations for strategy
- Human Player: Fuel Missed/Scored, Actions, Notes

After the match, click "Save Match". The data is saved to the History tab and as a local CSV file. Sync the data to Google Sheets or have the Head Scout scan the QR code. Then delete the match from history.

### Pit Scouting 
Switch to the "Pit" tab and fill out all fields for each team:
- Team Number, Climb Level, Height/Weight, Intake Type, Drive Mechanism, General Strategy, Features, Hopper Type & Capacity, Drive Experience, Build Quality, Outside Perimeter, Breakdowns, Notes

### History
- View all saved match and pit scout data
- Click an entry to see its QR code 
- Click the edit button to modify or delete saved data

## Troubleshooting
- If saved data doesn't appear, refresh the History tab
- For syncing issues, check your internet connection and try again in 30 seconds
- Ensure screen brightness is at 100% if QR codes won't scan
- If the wrong team was scouted, edit the team number in the History tab
